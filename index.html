From three founders
to Botswana's shelves

Registered in 2017 and fully operational since 2022, FoodForage was built to close a real gap: too much of Botswana's food is imported, while local ingredients go underused. So we manufacture our own — sourcing peanuts, ginger, beetroot and cucumber regionally and turning them into products you'll recognise on the shelf.

We're based in Old Naledi, Gaborone, and every jar and bottle that leaves our factory carries that same goal: proving that food made in Botswana can stand shoulder to shoulder with anything imported.
